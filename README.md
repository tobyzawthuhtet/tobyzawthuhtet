## 🧠 Thesis & Research

📌 In January 2025, I **successfully defended my Master’s thesis**, titled:

> *“In-Season Resilient Land Cover and Crop Feature Extraction and Classification”*  

This work explored advanced convolutional neural networks (1D, 2D, 3D CNNs) with customized Day-Of-Year input modules to improve seasonal classification of land cover and crop types, and conducted detailed analysis of model behavior across temporal and spectral settings.:contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

My research integrates AI with geospatial science to extract robust features from satellite time-series and supports generalizable models across different geographic regions.
